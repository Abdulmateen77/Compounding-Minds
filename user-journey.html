What the user does here

There are four ways to interact, and no chat box.

Pick

Choose from a list.

Company from the 50 we have loaded, shown as an anonymous ID with its sector.

Quarter, on a slider from 2018Q1 to 2024Q4.

View: Learned agents or their Frozen twins.

Press

Buttons that move things along.

Play, Step, and speed.

Run live loop for one company and quarter.

Reveal company name, after the calls are shown.

Click

Open what is behind a number.

An agent's row, to see its evidence.

A wrong call, to see its correction and the Mubit update.

Type

Only in two places.

A search box that filters the company list.

Live today tab (stretch): any US-listed ticker, for a view that cannot be graded yet.

It is a dashboard, not a chat. The committee answers six fixed questions, so there is nothing to ask it in free text. That is also what lets every answer be marked against real filings later.

Who decides what

The person chooses what to look at. The system forms views and marks itself. Nothing here tells anyone to buy or sell.

DecisionWho makes itHow
Which company and quarter to look atThe userPicks from the list and the slider
Whether to act on anything shownThe userThe tool never says buy or sell
Every number (cash flow, margins, coverage)PythonThe metrics engine, each number traced to a filing
Each call (yes, no or a bucket, plus a probability)The agentsLLMs judging those numbers against their investor's rules
The committee verdictThe synthesizerPython sets the weights from each agent's track record; an LLM writes the rationale
Whether a call was right or wrongThe graderPython comparing the call with a later filing. No LLM
What an agent learnsMubitUpdates only the agent that erred, from corrections the grader flagged
