# Buffett moat principles (own words, DRAFT, stretch agent: verify each citation before the demo)

moat.02 | Pricing power means margins hold even when revenue growth slows | pricing_power_proxy above zero | Buffett on pricing power as the key test of a business (2010 FCIC interview)
moat.03 | A steady downward margin trend is erosion, not noise | gross_margin_trend_8q below zero leans no | Berkshire letters, on moats that narrow over time
